About the studio

Designing spaces. Defining experiences.

Envoid Design Studio is a multidisciplinary architecture and interior design practice based in Vaishali, Ghaziabad, working across Delhi NCR. We design functional, timeless spaces for residential, commercial and institutional clients, and we stay involved through execution so the built result matches the drawing.

Our work starts with how people will actually use a space — light, movement, storage, privacy, budget — and resolves the architecture around that. Bold where it earns its place, quiet everywhere else.

Most of our work sits in Ghaziabad and Noida, from plot studies and GDA map approval through to interiors and handover.

Capabilities

One studio, end to end

Engage us for a single stage or hand over the whole project. The team, drawings and accountability stay in one place.

  • Architecture & Structure

    Concept design, planning, structural coordination and the drawing set your contractor can actually build from.

  • Interior Design

    Layouts, joinery, materials, lighting and styling — resolved to a specification, not a mood board.

  • Design & Build

    Design and construction under one contract: one point of responsibility, fewer gaps between intent and site.

  • Turnkey Execution

    Vendors, procurement, site supervision and finishing managed to handover — you receive a space ready to use.

  • Project Management

    Schedules, budgets, approvals and quality checks tracked and reported, with escalation before problems set in concrete.

  • Map Approval

    Building plan sanction handled end to end — drawings prepared to local development authority norms, submitted, followed up and revised until approval.

  • Vastu

    Vastu principles integrated into the plan from the start, balanced against orientation, structure and liveability.

  • Daylight Analysis

    Sun path and daylight studies that inform openings, shading and orientation — comfort and lower running costs by design.

  • Sustainable & Energy-Efficient Building

    Orientation, ventilation, insulation and material choices that cut cooling loads and running costs, with solar and rainwater provisioning where the site allows.

How we work

Four stages, no surprises

  1. 01

    Consult

    Site visit, requirements, budget band and timeline. You leave with a clear scope and fee.

  2. 02

    Design

    Concept options, 3D views and revisions until the plan is right — before anything is costed or ordered.

  3. 03

    Document

    Working drawings, specifications and BOQ. Approvals and vendor selection run in parallel.

  4. 04

    Execute

    Supervised construction and fit-out with scheduled site reports, snag list and handover.

What clients say

In the words of the people we built for.

    FAQ

    Questions people ask before they call

    The ones that come up on nearly every first call. Anything not here, ask us directly.

    Where is the studio, and which areas do you work in?

    We work from 366, Brahmaputra Lane, Sector 5, Vaishali, Ghaziabad. Most of our projects are across Ghaziabad — Vaishali, Indirapuram, Vasundhara, Kaushambi, Sahibabad, Raj Nagar Extension — and in Noida and Greater Noida, with the rest of Delhi NCR regularly and projects delivered as far as Lucknow, Kanpur, Hardoi and Bihar.

    How do you charge, and what does the first meeting cost?

    The first consultation is free, and includes a site visit where that is practical. After it you get a written scope and fee before any work starts. The fee depends on how far through the project you want us — a sanction drawing set, a full design, or design and execution under one contract are three different pieces of work, and we will not quote a rate before seeing the site.

    Do you handle GDA map approval?

    Yes, end to end: the plot and zone check, drawings to the authority's norms, filing, follow-up and revisions until the plan is sanctioned. How map approval works sets out the process and what happens after the sanction letter.

    We have a flat, not a plot. Do you take that on?

    Yes. Apartment interiors are a large part of our work and need no sanction, though your society will have its own fit-out rules and we plan around them. See interior design in Ghaziabad.

    Do you build as well as design?

    Both, together or separately. Under a design-and-build contract one studio is answerable for the gap between the drawing and what is actually built, which is where most disputes on a site live. You can equally take our drawings and build through your own contractor.

    How long will my project take?

    It depends on the scope, the approvals and how quickly decisions get made — and the honest answer is a schedule in writing with the quotation, reported against as the work runs, rather than a number on a web page. The thing that most reliably adds weeks is deciding materials late.

    Bring us the plot, the floor plan or just the idea.
