About the studio

Designing spaces. Defining experiences.

Envoid Design Studio is a multidisciplinary architecture and interior design practice based in Vaishali, Ghaziabad, working across Delhi NCR. We design functional, timeless spaces for residential, commercial and institutional clients, and we stay involved through execution so the built result matches the drawing.

Our work starts with how people will actually use a space — light, movement, storage, privacy, budget — and resolves the architecture around that. Bold where it earns its place, quiet everywhere else.

Capabilities

One studio, end to end

Engage us for a single stage or hand over the whole project. The team, drawings and accountability stay in one place.

  • Architecture & Structure

    Concept design, planning, structural coordination and the drawing set your contractor can actually build from.

  • Interior Design

    Layouts, joinery, materials, lighting and styling — resolved to a specification, not a mood board.

  • Design & Build

    Design and construction under one contract: one point of responsibility, fewer gaps between intent and site.

  • Turnkey Execution

    Vendors, procurement, site supervision and finishing managed to handover — you receive a space ready to use.

  • Project Management

    Schedules, budgets, approvals and quality checks tracked and reported, with escalation before problems set in concrete.

  • Map Approval

    Building plan sanction handled end to end — drawings prepared to local development authority norms, submitted, followed up and revised until approval.

  • Vastu

    Vastu principles integrated into the plan from the start, balanced against orientation, structure and liveability.

  • Daylight Analysis

    Sun path and daylight studies that inform openings, shading and orientation — comfort and lower running costs by design.

  • Sustainable & Energy-Efficient Building

    Orientation, ventilation, insulation and material choices that cut cooling loads and running costs, with solar and rainwater provisioning where the site allows.

  • Not sure what you need?

    Send us the site details and we will tell you the shortest route to a buildable design.

How we work

Four stages, no surprises

  1. 01

    Consult

    Site visit, requirements, budget band and timeline. You leave with a clear scope and fee.

  2. 02

    Design

    Concept options, 3D views and revisions until the plan is right — before anything is costed or ordered.

  3. 03

    Document

    Working drawings, specifications and BOQ. Approvals and vendor selection run in parallel.

  4. 04

    Execute

    Supervised construction and fit-out with scheduled site reports, snag list and handover.
