How the numbers are worked out

These are the same formulas the studio uses when it builds a quantity estimate, applied to the outline you draw.

  • Floor area is measured from the outline itself, so L-shapes, T-shapes and odd corners count correctly rather than being rounded to a rectangle.
  • Wall area is the perimeter times the ceiling height. Paintable walls take away every door and window.
  • Paint is the surface divided by the coverage of one litre for one coat, times the number of coats, plus your wastage.
  • Floor tiles are the floor area plus wastage, divided by the area of one tile, rounded up to whole tiles.
  • Skirting runs the full perimeter, less the width of each door.

What it does not know

Paint coverage differs from one brand, finish and wall to another, so take the figure from the tin rather than trusting the default here. A new or porous wall drinks more than a repaint. Tile counts follow the floor area; a diagonal or patterned layout, or a room with many short walls, cuts more tiles than plain wastage allows for. Treat every figure as a planning estimate. The quantity you order should come from a measurement taken on site.
