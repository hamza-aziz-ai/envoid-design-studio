      What "from" means

      Every figure here is the lowest the studio charges for that piece of work, on a straightforward plot. A larger or irregular plot, a complicated design, more floors than a typical house, or extra rounds of changes all add to it. Nothing here is a quotation: the actual fee is set out in writing, with the scope, before any work starts.

      The estimator covers drawing work only. GDA map approval, interior design and turnkey execution depend so much on the project that they are quoted after a visit rather than priced here.

      What each drawing is for

      • Floor plan sets out every room, wall and opening with dimensions. It is the drawing everything else is built on, which is why it is priced per floor.
      • Structural drawings show how the building stands up: foundations, columns, beams and slabs, for your contractor and for the authority.
      • Electrical and plumbing drawings place every point, run and fixture before walls are plastered, which is far cheaper than moving them after.
      • Elevation and 3D drawing fix how the building will look from outside, so the facade is agreed on paper rather than discovered on site.
