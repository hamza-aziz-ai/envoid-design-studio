The Marriage Hall

Bihar · Institutional · Elevations & structure

An institutional hall where the elevation carries the brief. A long frontage has to read as an occasion from the road, so it is composed as a columned order with deep shading to the openings and a corner that turns without losing the rhythm. Elevation and structure were designed together, which is what lets the spans a column-free hall needs sit behind a facade that was composed around them.

Farm House

Noida · Residential · Architecture & interiors

A farm house planned around its grounds rather than dropped onto them: the lawn, the gazebo and the pergola approach are part of the plan, not landscaping added afterwards. Covered terraces and a deep-set balcony do the climate work and keep the living spaces shaded through the afternoon. The lounge interiors were taken through with the architecture, so inside and outside were resolved as one project rather than two.

Residences

Lucknow · Kanpur · Hardoi · Sadrauna · Noida

Independent houses across Lucknow, Kanpur, Hardoi, Sadrauna and Noida, from a 2,300 sq ft residence to multi-storey elevations — some photographed after completion, others shown as renders. The architectural language moves with the client, from timber screens and a restrained contemporary front to a classical order, but the planning underneath does not: how the plot is used, where light enters, where storage goes, and how the elevation resolves a narrow frontage.

Interiors

Lucknow & Kanpur · Turnkey fit-out

Completed turnkey fit-out, photographed rather than rendered: bedrooms, dining, living and bath taken from layout through joinery, materials and lighting to handover. The kind of work where the drawing set matters most, because every millimetre of it was built by somebody reading one.
