Ghaziabad is not one market, and treating it as one is how briefs go wrong. The Trans-Hindon belt — Vaishali, Vasundhara, Indirapuram, Kaushambi, Sahibabad — is largely apartments and tightly packed plots, where most of the work is interior planning, elevation treatment on a narrow frontage, and making a compact footprint feel generous. Across the Hindon, Raj Nagar, Raj Nagar Extension and Govindpuram carry more independent plots, which brings setbacks, ground coverage, parking and a sanctioned plan into play from the first sketch.

What building in Ghaziabad actually involves

Construction on a plot here needs a building plan sanctioned by the Ghaziabad Development Authority. GDA runs an online building plan approval system, which checks a submitted drawing against the applicable rules before it reaches a human, so a drawing that ignores a setback or a coverage limit does not quietly pass — it is rejected by the software.

A few consequences worth knowing before you commission drawings:

  • What you may build is decided before design begins. Ground coverage, floor area ratio, setbacks and height follow from the plot's size and the zone it falls in under the master plan. Designing first and checking later is the most expensive sequence available.
  • The sanction is a planning permission, not a title. A sanctioned plan says the building may be built; it says nothing about who owns the land.
  • The sanction expires. Sanction letters carry a validity date, so a plan passed and then left for years may need to be taken back to the authority.
  • A completion certificate comes before occupancy. Under the building bye-laws the authority expects a completion certificate to be obtained before the building is occupied.
  • Your architect is named at the site. Sanction conditions require a board at the site carrying the permit number, the approval and validity dates and the architect's name — so the person who drew it is on record.

Rules change, and the authority is the only authority on them. We check the current position for your plot and zone at the start of a project rather than working from what was true last year.

What we do on a Ghaziabad project

  • Plot study. What the site allows, what the bye-laws allow, and where those two disagree with the brief.
  • Concept and planning. Options in plan and 3D, revised until the layout is right — before anything is costed.
  • Sanction drawings. The submission set prepared to the authority's norms, filed, followed up and revised until approval.
  • Working drawings and structure. The set a contractor can build from, with structural coordination rather than a separate, unreconciled design.
  • Interiors. Layouts, joinery, materials and lighting resolved to a specification.
  • Execution. Vendors, supervision and a snag list through to handover, for clients who would rather hand over the whole thing.

Engage us for one stage or all of them. The drawings, the team and the accountability stay in one place either way.

FAQ

Questions we are asked

Do you take on a single house, or only large projects?

A single plot is ordinary work for us, and most of our residential portfolio is exactly that. The scope scales down cleanly: a small house still gets a plot study, a sanctioned plan, a working drawing set and site visits, just fewer of each.

Do you handle GDA map approval yourself?

Yes. Map approval is one of our services. We prepare the submission drawings to the development authority's norms, file them, follow up and revise until the plan is sanctioned, so you are not carrying paperwork between an architect and the authority.

What we cannot promise is an outcome the rules do not allow. If the plot will not support what you have in mind, you hear that at the plot study, not after a rejection.

We own a flat, not a plot. Is that still your work?

Yes. In Vaishali, Indirapuram, Vasundhara and Crossings Republik most of what we do is interiors — planning, joinery, materials, lighting and execution inside an existing shell. There is no sanction involved, though the society usually has its own fit-out rules and we work to those.

How often do you visit the site?

On projects we are executing, on a schedule agreed in advance, with written site reports rather than a verbal update. On design-only engagements we visit at the stages where a drawing meets reality: setting out, slab, and before finishes are ordered.

How do you charge?

It depends on the scope and how far through the project you want us — a sanction drawing set, a full design, or design and execution under one contract are three different pieces of work. We quote after the first consultation and site visit, with the scope and the fee written down before anything starts. We do not quote a rate before seeing the site.

The first consultation itself is free.

Do you work outside Ghaziabad?

Yes. We work across Delhi NCR — Noida, Greater Noida, Delhi, Faridabad, Gurugram and Meerut — and have delivered projects well beyond it, in Lucknow, Kanpur, Hardoi and Bihar.
