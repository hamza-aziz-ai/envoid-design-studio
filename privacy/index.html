Privacy policy

What this website and our Android app collect, who it is sent to, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted. It is short because we collect very little, and specific because a policy that says "we value your privacy" and nothing else is worth nothing.

Who this is about

Envoid Design Architect Studio, 366, Brahmaputra Lane, Sector 5, Vaishali, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh 201019, India. We are an architecture and interior design practice. This policy covers two things we operate: the website at envoiddesign.in and the Envoid Design Studio Android app (in.envoiddesign.app).

Anything you can reach by tapping a link out of either one — WhatsApp, your phone's dialler, your email app, Google Maps, our Google reviews — belongs to whoever runs it, and their privacy policy applies there, not ours.

What we never do

No advertising pixel, no heatmap or session recording, no advertising identifier, no cross-site or cross-app tracking, and no profiling. We do not sell, rent or share personal information with anyone for their own purposes. The app has no analytics at all. The website has one analytics tool, Google Analytics, and it stays off unless you switch it on; the next section says exactly what it does.

Neither the website nor the app has accounts or logins, so there is no password and no profile.

Google Analytics, only if you accept

The first time you visit, a small panel asks whether we may use Google Analytics. Until you press Accept, nothing from Google Analytics loads: no script, no request and no cookie. If you press Decline, nothing is ever sent.

If you accept, it tells us which pages and design tools people use, how they arrived (a search, a link, typed in), the kind of device and browser, an approximate location at city level, whether someone tapped to call, WhatsApp or email us, and that an enquiry was sent, with the service asked about. It never receives what you type into the enquiry form: not your name, phone number, email address or brief. Google Analytics does not store IP addresses.

It sets two cookies on this site, _ga and _ga_95C4HE9ZZC, which hold a random identifier so that two page views can be counted as one visit; they expire after two years. Google processes the data for us under its Analytics terms, and it may be processed outside India. We have switched off Google signals and advertising personalisation, the property is not linked to any advertising account, and the data is kept for 14 months and then deleted.

Your choice is remembered in your own browser, not in a cookie. You can change it at any time with Cookie settings at the foot of every page; declining after accepting switches analytics off at once and deletes its cookies.

The enquiry form on this website

The form at envoiddesign.in/contact is the only place the website asks you for anything. It collects what you type: your name, phone number and email address, which are required, and the service you want, your city or site location and your project brief, which are optional. Nothing is sent until you press Send.

We use it to answer your enquiry, and for the record of having answered it. It reaches us as two emails handled by EmailJS, our email delivery provider: one to the studio, and one confirmation copy to the address you typed, so you have proof of what you sent. If your browser has JavaScript switched off the form falls back to posting to FormSubmit, which forwards it to the same studio address instead. Both are processors acting for us.

One detail worth stating because it is not obvious. When you type an email address, the page checks that the domain can actually receive mail, so a typo does not silently lose your enquiry. That check is a DNS lookup sent to Cloudflare. Only the part after the @ leaves your browser — gmail.com, not your address — and if the lookup fails for any reason the form still sends.

Enquiries sit in the studio's email. We keep them while a project or conversation is live and for our own records afterwards; ask us and we will delete yours.

What happens simply by loading a page

Three things, and we would rather name them than let you assume the page is inert.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Like every web host, they log requests, which includes your IP address, for delivery and abuse prevention. We do not use those logs for analytics and we have no dashboard built on them.

The typefaces load from Google Fonts, so your IP address and browser details reach Google when a page is rendered. The enquiry page also loads the EmailJS script from jsDelivr, a public code network, with the same consequence. Neither is tracking you on our behalf, but both are requests to a third party that we chose to make, and you could not know that from looking at the page.

Client reviews, in the app and on this site

The app has a form for leaving a review of work we did for you. It collects only what you type, and only when you press Send: the name you want published, a rating from one to five, your review text, and optionally a short project description such as "Residence, Lucknow". It asks for no email address, no phone number and no account.

It is sent over HTTPS to our database, hosted by Supabase, and stored as pending. Nobody but the studio can read it in that state.

A person at the studio reads every review before it appears anywhere. If we approve it, the name, rating, project and text you wrote are published publicly — in the app and on this website. If we do not, it stays unpublished. So submitting a review is a request to publish what you wrote under the name you chose: please do not put anything in it you would not want public, and do not include anyone else's personal details. We publish approved reviews on the basis of that consent, and nothing else.

Reviews that are never approved are deleted periodically. Approved ones stay published until you ask us to take them down.

Separately, this site republishes reviews that clients left on our Google listing. Those were posted publicly on Google by their authors, we copy them unedited and credit Google, and we do not control them — to change or remove one, edit it on Google, where it was written.

What the app keeps on your phone

Your light or dark theme preference, and a copy of the already-published reviews so the screen still works with no signal. Both stay on the device, neither is sent anywhere, and uninstalling the app removes them.

Children

Neither the website nor the app is directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. Both exist to show an architecture practice's work and let someone get in touch about a building project.

How to see, correct or delete what we hold

Write to envoid.design.architect@gmail.com and we will tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it — including unpublishing a review you submitted. Please say enough for us to find it, such as the name on the review or the email address you sent an enquiry from. We answer within 30 days.

You can also write to the postal address at the top of this page, or call +91 95607 69743.

Changes to this policy

If it changes, the date at the top changes with it. We will not quietly start collecting something this page says we do not.
