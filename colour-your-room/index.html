    Why the light matters

    A colour is never just a colour. The same wall reads cool and bright at noon and warm and deep under a lamp at night, and a dark floor that grounds a room by day can swallow it after dark. Switch between daylight and evening with a few combinations and you will see why a sample on the wall, looked at morning and night, beats a chip in a shop.

    A feature wall works best behind the thing you want the eye to land on, here the sofa, and a darker one makes a room feel more enclosed, which is pleasant in a large room and heavy in a small one. Try the charcoal and the deep green on the feature wall and watch the room change size.

    This is a quick sketch with flat colours, not a rendering. Real finishes bring texture, sheen and grain that a flat swatch cannot show, which is why the studio puts physical samples in front of you before anything is ordered.
