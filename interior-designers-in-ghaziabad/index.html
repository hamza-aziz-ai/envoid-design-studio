Interior designers in Ghaziabad

Space planning, joinery, materials, lighting and turnkey execution for flats and houses across Ghaziabad — from a studio in Sector 5, Vaishali.

Two very different jobs under one name

Interior work in Ghaziabad splits almost cleanly in two, and the difference decides everything about how a project runs.

A builder flat in Indirapuram, Vaishali, Vasundhara or Crossings Republik arrives finished to a standard that suits nobody in particular: a kitchen with one counter, bedrooms without storage, and lighting placed where the wiring was convenient. The work is planning and joinery inside walls that are not moving, under a society's fit-out rules, against a hard constraint on how long a service lift can be blocked.

An independent house carries none of those limits and all of the others. Walls can move, services can be re-run, levels can change — which means the interior design has to be reconciled with the structure rather than fitted around it, and it usually wants to start before the building is finished rather than after.

What we actually hand over

A mood board is not a design. What leaves our studio is a specification: drawings a carpenter can set out from, a materials schedule with makes and finishes named, a lighting layout with circuits, and a bill of quantities. That is what makes a quotation comparable between vendors and an argument on site short.

  • Space planning. Circulation, storage, sight lines and daylight before anything is styled.
  • Joinery drawings. Wardrobes, kitchens, vanities, TV units and storage, drawn to the millimetre rather than ordered from a catalogue photograph.
  • Materials and finishes. Named and sampled — laminates, veneers, stone, tile, paint, hardware — chosen for a climate that is hot, dusty for much of the year, and humid in monsoon.
  • Lighting. Layered, with circuits and switching planned, so a room has more than one setting.
  • Electrical and plumbing coordination. Point layouts agreed before a wall is chased, not after.
  • Execution. Vendors, procurement, supervision, snag list and handover, if you want the whole thing managed.

Working inside a society

Most apartment complexes here place real conditions on fit-out: written permission before work starts, a security deposit, fixed working hours, restrictions on which lift carries material, rules on debris removal, and often a bar on any work that touches the structure. None of it is unreasonable and all of it affects the schedule. We plan around those rules from the start, because discovering them in week two is how a six-week job becomes a four-month one.

What it costs, honestly

Anyone quoting a per-square-foot number for your flat before seeing it is quoting an average, not a price. The spread between a sensible fit-out and an indulgent one is several times over, and it is driven by material choices and the amount of joinery, not by floor area. We give you a budget band at the first consultation, and a bill of quantities you can price against before you commit.

We are equally happy to design only, and let you execute through your own contractor. The specification is written to be built from by anyone competent.

FAQ

Questions we are asked

What does interior design include, exactly?

Space planning, joinery drawings, a materials and finishes schedule, a lighting plan, electrical and plumbing point layouts, and a bill of quantities. Execution — vendors, procurement, supervision and handover — is available on top, and is a separate scope.

Styling and loose furniture can be part of it or not, as you prefer.

Can we hire you for just the kitchen or the wardrobes?

Yes. A single-room or single-element scope is fine, though we will say if the rest of the flat makes that choice harder than it needs to be.

Do you handle the society's fit-out permission?

We prepare what the society asks for and work to its conditions on hours, lifts and debris. The application is usually made in the owner's name, so you sign it, but you are not working out what to attach.

How long does a flat take?

It depends on the amount of joinery and how much is site-made rather than factory-made, and on the society's working-hour rules. We put a schedule in writing with the quotation, and report against it rather than asking you to chase.

The one thing that reliably adds weeks is deciding materials late. We try to close those choices before work starts.

Will you buy the materials, or do we?

Either. On a turnkey scope we procure and you approve the samples; on a design-only scope we hand you a specification precise enough to buy against. What we will not do is leave the choice vague enough that a vendor substitutes something cheaper.

Can you work with the contractor we already have?

Yes, and it is common. The drawing set is written to be built from by any competent contractor, and we are happy to answer their queries during the work.
