Guess the area

Five rooms, each drawn with its wall lengths the way a floor plan shows them. Guess the floor area in square feet: the closer you get, the more points.

·

Your guess

Why this is harder than it looks

Most people misjudge floor area by a long way, and the error matters: it is the number a flat is sold by, a contractor quotes by and a tile order is worked out from. A rectangle is easy. An L-shape or a room with a bay in it is where guesses drift, because the eye counts the outline rather than the floor.

The quick way is to split the room into rectangles, work out each and add them up. Or take the whole outer rectangle and subtract the missing corner. After each guess the room fills with one-foot squares, so you can count the answer for yourself.

Square feet, carpet and built-up

The floor inside the walls is the carpet area, which is what this game asks for. A built-up figure adds the walls themselves, and a super built-up one adds a share of lifts, lobbies and stairs, which is why the same flat can be advertised at three very different sizes.
