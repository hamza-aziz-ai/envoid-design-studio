Architects in Noida & Greater Noida

Plots, farm houses, builder floors and apartments — drawn to the relevant authority's regulations, built with supervision.

Across the Hindon, a different rulebook

Noida and Greater Noida sit a short drive from our studio in Vaishali, and we work there regularly — but they are not Ghaziabad administratively, and that is the part clients are most often surprised by. Plots in Noida fall under the New Okhla Industrial Development Authority; Greater Noida and the Noida Extension belt fall under the Greater Noida Industrial Development Authority. Each runs its own building regulations, its own sanction process and its own conditions on what a plot allows.

Most Noida plots are also leasehold and allotted by the authority, which brings conditions of its own — on use, on the time allowed to complete construction, and on changes to the sanctioned plan. A design that ignores the allotment terms can be perfectly good architecture and still unbuildable.

How we run a Noida project

  • Read the allotment and the regulations first. What the lease and the authority permit on your plot, before a line is drawn.
  • Concept and planning, in plan and 3D, revised until it is right.
  • Sanction drawings prepared to the relevant authority's norms, submitted and followed through revisions.
  • Working drawings and structural coordination — a set a contractor can build from without interpreting.
  • Interiors and execution, as a separate stage or under one design-and-build contract.

Being close enough to visit matters more on an authority-governed plot than almost anywhere else, because queries come back during the sanction process and a slow answer costs weeks.

FAQ

Questions we are asked

Is approval in Noida the same as GDA map approval?

No. Ghaziabad plots are sanctioned by the Ghaziabad Development Authority; Noida plots by the Noida authority and Greater Noida plots by the Greater Noida authority, each under its own building regulations. The work we do is the same in shape — a compliant submission set, filed and followed up — but the rules it is drawn to are different, and we confirm the current ones for your plot at the start.

Do you work in Greater Noida and Noida Extension?

Yes, both, as well as the sector plots in Noida proper.

We have an authority-allotted plot with a completion deadline. Can you work to it?

Yes, and it should shape the programme from day one. We plan the design and sanction stages backwards from the deadline rather than discovering it late.

Can you do interiors only, in an apartment?

Yes. Apartment interiors need no sanction, and we take them on in Noida on the same terms as in Ghaziabad.

How far do you travel for site visits?

Across Delhi NCR as standard. We have also delivered projects in Lucknow, Kanpur, Hardoi and Bihar, where visits are scheduled in blocks around construction milestones rather than weekly.
