Interior design

Layouts, joinery, materials, lighting and styling — resolved to a specification, not a mood board.

A specification, not a mood board

Interior design is frequently sold as a set of pretty images and a per-square-foot rate. Neither builds anything. What a carpenter, an electrician and a stone supplier need is dimensioned drawings, named materials and measured quantities, and that is what we produce.

What the service covers

  • Space planning. Circulation, storage, sight lines, privacy and daylight — resolved before anything is styled.
  • Joinery drawings. Wardrobes, kitchens, vanities, storage and built-in furniture drawn to the millimetre, with hardware named.
  • Materials and finishes. Laminates, veneers, stone, tile, paint and hardware, specified by make and finish and sampled before approval.
  • Lighting design. Layered lighting with circuits and switching planned, so a room has more than one mood and the wiring supports it.
  • Services coordination. Electrical points, plumbing and air-conditioning positions agreed before a wall is chased.
  • Bill of quantities. So vendor quotations are comparable line by line.
  • Styling and handover, where you want the space finished rather than merely built.

Built for the way these spaces are actually used

Storage is the single most under-designed thing in Indian homes, and the first thing clients regret. So is the kitchen triangle, the service balcony nobody drew, the puja space added at the end, and the one socket that ends up behind a bed. Our planning starts from how the household runs — who cooks, who studies where, who visits, what gets stored seasonally — and the finishes follow that rather than leading it.

Material choices are made for a dusty, hot, humid-in-monsoon climate: finishes that can be cleaned, surfaces that do not show every mark, and joinery detailed so that swelling in monsoon does not jam a drawer.

Design only, or design and execute

You can take the drawing set and build it through your own contractor — it is written to be built from by anyone competent. Or we can execute it: vendors, procurement, supervision, snag list, handover. On a turnkey scope you approve samples and we carry the coordination.

FAQ

Questions we are asked

Do you work on flats as well as houses?

Both, and they are different jobs. A builder flat is planning and joinery inside walls that are not moving, under a society's fit-out rules. A house lets the interior and the structure be designed together, which is usually better and needs to start earlier.

Can we use you for the design and our own contractor for the work?

Yes, and many clients do. The specification is deliberately precise enough to be executed by someone else, and we will answer your contractor's queries during the work.

How do you quote?

After seeing the space and understanding the scope — never before. A per-square-foot number quoted sight unseen is an average of other people's projects, not a price for yours. You get a budget band at the consultation and a BOQ to price against before committing.

Do you do modular kitchens and wardrobes?

Yes, both modular and site-made, and we will tell you which suits your space, budget and timeline rather than defaulting to whichever we sell.

What about Vastu?

We integrate Vastu where a client wants it, from the planning stage, balanced against orientation, structure and liveability. It works far better as an input to the layout than as a correction to a finished one.
