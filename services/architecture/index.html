Architecture & structure

Plot study, concept, elevation, structural coordination and the working drawing set your contractor can actually build from.

Drawings that survive contact with a site

An architectural design is only as good as the set of drawings it ends up as. A beautiful render that leaves the contractor guessing at a junction detail will be resolved on site, badly, by whoever is standing there that morning. Our documentation exists to stop that happening.

What the service covers

  • Site and plot study. What the plot allows physically and what the bye-laws allow legally — ground coverage, floor area ratio, setbacks, height, parking — set against your brief, with the conflicts named early.
  • Concept design. Options in plan, massing and 3D. Revisions until the layout is right, while changing it still costs nothing but time.
  • Elevation and material treatment. How the building meets the street, in a climate where south and west faces decide the cooling bill.
  • Structural coordination. Spans, column positions and slab layouts reconciled with the architecture rather than imposed on it afterwards.
  • Working drawings. Dimensioned plans, sections, elevations and details, plus electrical and plumbing layouts, at a resolution a contractor can set out from.
  • Specifications and BOQ. Named materials and measured quantities, so quotations are comparable and a vendor cannot substitute quietly.
  • Sanction set. The submission drawings for the development authority, where the project needs approval.

Designing for this climate, not a magazine

Delhi NCR is hot for a long stretch, dusty for most of the year and humid in monsoon, and the power bill of a badly oriented house says so every summer. Orientation, shading, cross ventilation, wall and roof build-ups and glazing choices do more for comfort than any mechanical system bolted on later, and they cost far less when they are decided in the concept rather than retrofitted.

Where it is useful, we run sun-path and daylight studies so those choices are made against numbers rather than instinct. You can get a feel for it with our sunlight explorer.

Residential, commercial, institutional

The studio works across all three. Our portfolio includes independent residences from compact plots upwards, a farm house with its own site planning and pavilions, and an institutional marriage hall where the elevation and structure were both ours. Different programmes, the same discipline.

FAQ

Questions we are asked

We have a plot but no idea what it can take. Can you tell us?

That is the plot study, and it is usually the right first step. It establishes what the rules permit on that plot and in that zone, what the shape and orientation make sensible, and whether the brief fits inside both. It is far cheaper than discovering the answer through a rejected drawing.

Do you provide structural drawings too?

We coordinate the structure with the architecture and deliver a set that works together. Where a project needs a specialist structural design — a long span, an unusual frame, a difficult soil — that is brought in and reconciled with our drawings rather than handed over separately.

Can you work from an existing plan we already have?

Yes. We are often brought in to take a sanctioned or half-finished plan to a buildable set, or to redesign an elevation over a layout that is already fixed. We will tell you plainly if the existing plan limits what can be done.

How many design revisions do we get?

Enough to get it right. The number is written into the scope so nobody is guessing, and in practice the revisions that matter happen early, at concept, when they are free.

Do you also build what you design?

If you want us to. Design and build under one contract removes the gap between the drawing and the site, and that is a separate service page.
