One studio, end to end

Most projects go wrong in the joints: between the architect and the structural engineer, between the drawing and the contractor, between what was designed and what was finally built. We are organised to remove those joints. The same studio can take a project from the first look at a plot to the day you get the keys, and the drawings, the team and the accountability stay in one place throughout.

Engage us for a single stage or hand over the whole thing. Both are ordinary.

How a project usually runs

  1. Consult. Site visit, requirements, budget band and timeline. You leave with a scope and a fee.
  2. Design. Concept options, 3D views and revisions until the plan is right — before anything is costed or ordered.
  3. Document. Working drawings, specifications and a bill of quantities. Approvals and vendor selection run in parallel.
  4. Execute. Supervised construction and fit-out, scheduled site reports, a snag list, and handover.

Everything else we do

Beyond the four services with pages of their own, the studio also handles project management — schedules, budgets, approvals and quality checks tracked and reported, with escalation before a problem sets in concrete; Vastu, integrated into the plan from the start and balanced against orientation, structure and liveability rather than bolted on; daylight analysis, sun-path and daylight studies that inform openings, shading and orientation; and sustainable, energy-efficient building, where orientation, ventilation, insulation and material choices cut cooling loads and running costs, with solar and rainwater provisioning where the site allows.

Not sure which of these you need? Send us the site details and we will tell you the shortest route to a buildable design.

FAQ

Questions we are asked

Can we engage you for one stage only?

Yes. A plot study, a sanction drawing set, a concept design, a working drawing set or an interior specification are all available on their own. Clients frequently start with one stage and continue because the next one is easier with the same drawings behind it.

What happens at the first consultation?

A site visit where possible, your requirements, a budget band and a timeline. You leave with a clear scope and a fee — not a proposal to be chased. The consultation is free.

Do you design and also build?

Both, together or separately. Under a design-and-build contract one party is answerable for the gap between the drawing and the thing built, which is where most disputes on a site actually live.

Which services do you provide beyond design?

Map approval, project management, Vastu-informed planning, daylight analysis, and sustainable and energy-efficient design — orientation, ventilation, insulation and material choices, with solar and rainwater provisioning where the site allows.
